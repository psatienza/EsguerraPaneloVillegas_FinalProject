FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHAT IS BOOKMARK PH?

Bookmark PH is a book club for young adults who want to discover stories, share ideas, and connect with fellow readers through discussions and gatherings.

IS THERE A MEMBERSHIP FEE?

No. Joining Bookmark PH is free. Some events may have optional costs, which we'll share in advance.

CAN I JOIN BOOKMARK PH EVEN THOUGH I'M NOT FROM THE PHILIPPINES?

Yes! Readers from anywhere are welcome to join our online community. In-person gatherings take place in selected locations across the Philippines.

HOW TO BECOME A MEMBER?

Fill out our Google Form to sign up. After you submit it, we'll send you a message with confirmation and details about joining Bookmark PH.

WHAT ARE THE EVENTS SHOULD MEMBER EXPECT?

Expect relaxed, welcoming gatherings with book discussions, activities, and opportunities to meet other readers. You're welcome to participate at your own pace.
