ADVOCACY

We believe reading can open minds, build empathy, and bring people together. Bookmark PH creates welcoming spaces where young adults can explore diverse stories, share perspectives, and support a stronger reading culture across the Philippines.

HISTORY

Bookmark PH began with a simple idea: a book can bring strangers together. What started as a shared love of reading grew into a community where young adults discover books, exchange reflections, and meet at gatherings around the country. Every member adds a new mark to our story.

MISSION

Bookmark PH fosters an inclusive reading community for young adults across the Philippines through monthly book selections, thoughtful discussions, and locally hosted gatherings. We promote meaningful engagement with diverse stories and encourage a lasting culture of reading and connection.

VALUES & GUIDELINES

Community • Respect • Inclusivity • Curiosity • Integrity

Be respectful, welcome different perspectives, mark spoilers, keep discussions inclusive, and credit authors and sources.
